PROFILE

About

Dr. Talanki Jeevan Kumar is an academic in English with teaching and research interests spanning literature, language teaching, communication skills and technology-enabled education. His doctoral research, The Selected Plays of Harold Pinter: A Study, was completed at Sri Krishnadevaraya University, Ananthapuramu.

His academic work combines literary studies with English Language Teaching, communicative competence, phonetics, digital pedagogy, skill development and contemporary educational concerns.

Academic Distinctions

M.A. English Literature — Gold Medallist, Sri Krishnadevaraya University.

B.A. — University Second Rank.

Recipient of the Smt. Movva Venkata Lakshmi Rama Sarma Memorial Gold Medal and Smt. Vasagiri Kamalamma Memorial Prize.
